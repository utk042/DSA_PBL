PYQ Topic Frequency Analyser

Paste previous years' question papers. The analyser merges spelling variants, counts every topic in an AVL tree, ranks them with a max-heap, groups topics that are examined together, and picks the revision shortlist that fits your hours.

1–2 · Parser & normaliser

LCS · O(mn)

Each raw keyword is cleaned (case, punctuation, plurals, filler words), then compared with every known topic. similarity = LCS(a, b) / max(|a|, |b|); at or above the threshold the two are merged.

3 · Topic store

AVL tree · O(log n)

Node label = frequency. Hover a node for its topic. Scroll sideways on narrow screens.

Traversals

Topic-frequency table (in-order traversal — alphabetical)

4 · Ranking

Max-Heap · Top-N O(n + N log n)
Full ranked list (Heap Sort, O(n log n))

    5 · Co-occurrence graph

    Adjacency list · O(V + E)

    Edge = topics asked together; thicker = more often. Weight is 1 / count, so strong links are “short”. Colour = cluster. Kruskal MST edge Dijkstra path.

    Unit × unit view (fixed 5 × 5 adjacency matrix)

    6–7 · Clusters & revision routes

    DFS components · Kruskal · Prim

    Each connected component is a revision module. The route is Prim's order, started from the cluster's most-asked topic; Kruskal's backbone has the same total weight.
